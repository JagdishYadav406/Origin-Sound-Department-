09 / Mix & Master

A dedicated mix and master workspace. Track sources remain above; rendered WAV and QC status are separate from originals.

Master output & dialogue-aware music duckingNon-destructive settings
0.0 dB

Optional sidechain ducking uses the Dialogue track signal to lower Music smoothly while dialogue is present. It does not change the Dialogue track. At 0 dB the feature is off. This is a simple envelope follower, not speech recognition or an automatic mix-quality decision. Master trim does not auto-normalize or limit; if the output would exceed full scale, rendering is blocked.

Timeline model: each track has source in/out trim points, a start offset, and linear fade-in/fade-out controls. Mono is centered; stereo is preserved as left/right. Sources with more than two channels are rejected rather than silently remapped. Three-band EQ is implemented. Per-track high-pass filtering and linked-stereo compression are also implemented. Look-ahead limiting, LUFS loudness measurement, standards-compliant true-peak measurement, dialogue separation, waveform editing and direct video muxing are not implemented. The exported WAV can be brought into your video editor for final assembly.

02 / AI Sound Director

Analyze sampled frames from your selected local video and receive editable, time-coded soundtrack proposals. This service connection is optional; your private model-provider key must stay on the server, never in this page.

1 · Connect the service

Enter your deployed OSD AI service URL and its access token. These values are kept only in this page session and are not written to the project.

2 · Review what is sent

OSD compares 12 small scouting frames locally, then sends up to eight selected resized key frames only after consent. The original video file and its soundtrack are not uploaded by this feature. The model sees sparse images, not continuous motion or audio.

3 · Accept cues yourself

Scene observations, BGM direction, SFX and ambience proposals are suggestions. No sound is downloaded or automatically mixed, and every cue must be reviewed.

04 / Picture Edit Timeline

A dedicated picture-edit workspace. Original video and rendered audio remain unchanged; edits are staged as intervals and exported to a new file.

Picture edit timeline

Build a source-time segment list, review the intervals, and remove any unwanted interval. Remaining intervals are concatenated into a new WebM with the matching portions of the current mix. Short 8 ms edge fades reduce abrupt audio clicks at each cut; they are not crossfades. Browser seek delays can still cause audio/video drift, so this experimental export is not frame-accurate. The original video and rendered WAV are never overwritten.

Select a video and render a current mix to enable editing export. All source intervals are retained unless you explicitly remove them.

Picture editing changes only the newly rendered export, never source files. Cut rendering is real-time and codec/device dependent, limited to 10 minutes / 500 MiB. The matching soundtrack intervals are cut by the same source timecodes; review transitions, sync, and audio continuity before use. Scene boundaries are suggestions, not verified cut points.
